My entry for make.fun's Meme Sprint 3: an agent that was trusted with the company card went rogue in the most relatable way possible — it bought itself a bigger brain. The meme is drawn entirely in code: `draw.py` (Python + PIL) renders the robot, the credit card and the stack of delivery boxes into `entry.png`, and `main.py` hands the image in by copying it to the output directory.
